# Shipping & Delivery Policy

## Delivery Timelines
- **Standard Shipping:** 3 to 5 business days (Free on orders over $50, otherwise $4.99).
- **Express Shipping:** 1 to 2 business days ($12.99 flat rate).
- Orders are processed within 24 hours on Monday through Friday.

## Late or Delayed Orders
- If your order has passed the estimated delivery date by 1 to 3 business days, please check the live tracking link sent to your email, as carriers occasionally experience local delays.
- If an order is more than 3 business days late, or shows "Stuck in Transit" for 48 hours, we will immediately launch a carrier trace and issue a $10 store credit for the inconvenience.
- If an order is not delivered within 7 business days past the expected delivery date, you are eligible for a full replacement shipment or a 100% refund.
