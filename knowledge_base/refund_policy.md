# Refund, Return & Replacement Policy

## Return & Refund Window
- Customers may return most unused items within **30 calendar days** of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method.
- Footwear, apparel, and electronics must be in original packaging with all tags attached.

## How to Request a Return or Refund
1. Go to the **Orders** page in your account dashboard.
2. Select the item and click **Request Return / Refund**.
3. Download and print the prepaid return shipping label.
4. Drop the package at any authorized carrier drop-off location.
5. Refunds are processed within **5 to 7 business days** after our warehouse receives and inspects the item.

## Damaged or Broken Products (Replacements)
- If an item arrives damaged, defective, or broken (such as kitchen appliances, blenders, or electronics), you do **not** need to pay for return shipping.
- Report the damaged item within **14 days of delivery** with a photo of the damage and your Order ID.
- We will ship a brand-new replacement within **24 hours** at zero cost, or issue an immediate full refund if you prefer.
