# Product & Billing FAQ

## Warranty Coverage
- All electrical appliances (including blenders, mixers, and coffee makers) include a **1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty** covering motor defects and electrical failure.

## Payment Methods & Billing
- We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, PayPal, and UPI/NetBanking.
- **Duplicate Charges / Billing Disputes:** Our checkout system places a temporary authorization hold that may look like a double charge but drops within 48 hours. However, if your card was actually settled/charged multiple times, the ticket must be escalated immediately to our Senior Billing & Fraud Specialist team for manual ledger reversal.
