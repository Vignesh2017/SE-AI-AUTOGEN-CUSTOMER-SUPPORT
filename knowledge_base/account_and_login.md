# Account Management & Login Support

## How to Reset Your Password
1. Visit the Login page and click **Forgot Password?** below the sign-in button.
2. Enter the email address registered with your account.
3. Check your inbox (and Spam/Promotions folder) for the password reset link, which arrives within 2 minutes.
4. Click the link (valid for 15 minutes) and create a new password with at least 8 characters, one number, and one special symbol.

## Account Locked
- After 5 failed login attempts, your account is temporarily locked for 30 minutes for security.
- Completing a password reset immediately unlocks your account.
